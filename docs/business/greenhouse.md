# Greenhouse

Mosses collected in the herbarium grow in the pots of a greenhouse. The greenhouse never produces anything by itself, over time or otherwise: **dew comes only from completed tasks** (including the bonus of a shared goal they reach, SG4), and the bigger and richer the greenhouse, the more dew each task brings (XP is unchanged). Dew pays for facilities (more pots, misters, a rain barrel) and for expeditions that bring back new mosses. Important work done before it is urgent (Plant) feeds the greenhouse most.

- **GH1** Every player has one greenhouse, opened small: glasshouse level 1 (2 pots), misters and rain barrel level 0, no dew. — `Greenhouse::open()`, `SignInHandler`
- **GH2** Completing a task brings dew once per task (G1), straight to the dew balance: a base by quadrant (Plant 12, Water 6, Trim 3, Compost 1, unsorted 1) plus the greenhouse bonus of GH4. Streak and deadline do not change dew (they stay XP only). A subtask and the parent it completes each bring their own dew (S3). — `DewPolicy`, `GatherTaskDew`, `CompleteTaskHandler`
- **GH3** The **greenhouse yield** is the sum of its potted mosses by rarity (common 2, uncommon 3, rare 5, very rare 8 dew per task), raised by 10 % per misters level. It is counted in tenths of dew (the API's `yieldTenths`) and only floored once a task's bonus is computed. Empty pots yield nothing. — `Rarity::dewYield()`, `Pot::yield()`, `Greenhouse::yieldTenths()`
- **GH4** The greenhouse bonus depends on the quadrant, on a strict ladder: **Plant is watered** with the yield × (2 + rain barrel level); **Water gets half a watering**, half of that; **Trim is misted** with the yield × 1; Compost and unsorted tasks get no bonus. Each bonus is floored to whole dew per task. Whatever the yield, Plant > Water > Trim > Compost, and the gap grows with the greenhouse while Compost stays at 1. — `DewPolicy`, `Greenhouse::wateringMultiplier()`
- **GH5** Any collected moss can be planted in any pot, one pot per species at a time; planting into a pot that already holds a moss replaces it, and emptying a pot is free. A potted moss stays in the herbarium. Only mosses of one's own herbarium can be planted. Changes apply to the next completed task. — `Greenhouse::plant()`, `Greenhouse::unplant()`, `MossAlreadyPlanted`, `MossNotCollected`, `UnknownPot`, `PotIsEmpty`
- **GH6** Upgrades are instant, never timed: paying the cost of the next level raises it at once. Levels never drop. An unaffordable upgrade, or one beyond the last level, changes nothing. Costs to reach each level: — `Facility`, `Greenhouse::upgrade()`, `NotEnoughDew`, `FacilityAtMaxLevel`

- **GH7** An expedition costs 150 + 40·n + 8·n² dew (n = expeditions already made: 150, 198, 262, 342…) and brings back one species not collected yet, drawn like a level-up species (G7). It is refused, without cost, once the herbarium is complete. — `Greenhouse::fundExpedition()`, `Greenhouse::expeditionCost()`, `LaunchExpeditionHandler`, `HerbariumComplete`
- **GH8** Dew gathered over a lifetime (base and bonus of every task) never decreases; spending only lowers the balance. — `Greenhouse::dewGathered()`
- **GH9** The greenhouse has three achievements (G9): **Field trip** for the first expedition, **Morning dew** for 1,000 dew gathered over a lifetime (GH8), **Growing glasshouse** for a glasshouse at level 5. They are checked after completing a task, upgrading a facility and coming back from an expedition. — `FieldTrip`, `MorningDew`, `GrowingGlasshouse`, `DoctrinePlayerStatsLedger`, `AchievementCheck`
- **GH10** When the greenhouse was introduced, every player got one opened as in GH1, with the base dew of GH2 (no bonus) for every task already rewarded. — migration `Version20261009043059`
- **GH11** Changes to the same greenhouse made at the same moment (two devices, two tabs, a task completed while planting) are applied one after the other, each starting from what the previous one saved: dew is never spent or credited twice, a completion never fails because the greenhouse just changed, and a moss never sits in two pots (GH5). Two plantings into the same pot end with the one applied last, as if made in turn. — `GreenhouseRepository::lockedOf()`, `AtomicChange`, `greenhouse_pot_species` unique index (migration `Version20261009043059`)
