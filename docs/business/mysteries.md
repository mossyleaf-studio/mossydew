# Moss Mysteries

An opt-in logic puzzle fed by completed tasks. A generated board hides a murder: everyone stands somewhere, one person is the victim and the murderer is the only other person in the victim's room. The victim's clue is given from the start; the other clues stay hidden until tasks are completed, one clue per task. Solving a case unlocks achievements only (no XP, no dew). In the code a game is an `Investigation` (the `Mystery` context); `Case` is a reserved word in PHP.

- **MY1** Moss Mysteries is off by default and switched on in Settings › Preferences (`PUT /api/me/mysteries` `{enabled}`, saved on the account and in the session as `mysteries`, so it survives a reload). While it is off the Mysteries entry is hidden from the navigation and no clue is revealed, but an open case is kept as it is. — `User::playMysteries()`, `ChooseMysteriesHandler`, `SecurityUser`, `RevealClues`
- **MY2** A case is an N×N board, N drawn between 6 and 14, split into contiguous rooms of at least 2 cells (between ⌈N/2⌉ and N rooms), each of a different kind (kitchen, living room, bedroom…). N people live on it: N−1 suspects and 1 victim, known from the start. The victim's name starts with V and no suspect's does, so the victim is marked V on the board. Every name comes with a gender, female or male, sent with the case (`genders`, same order as `people`) so clues can say "she" or "he" and "alone with a woman". — `CaseGenerator`, `CaseGenerator::genderOf()`, `Gender`, `Floorplan`, `RoomKind`, `Blueprint`
- **MY3** Exactly one person stands on each row and on each column. Armchair, sofa and bed can hold a person; plant, books, bath, toilet, tv, piano, lamp, stove and washer block their cell, nobody stands there. About a quarter of the squares are furnished, at most 14% of them (rounded to the nearest square) with a blocking piece, and every room keeps at least 2 squares free of blocking pieces. The generator first spreads pieces over about 12% of each room (blocking pieces on at most 7% of the board), then adds the blocking pieces some clues point at, as long as the 14% allows (otherwise the case is drawn again), and finally fills each room up to about 25%, with seats only once the 14% is reached. It puts seats on any free cell and blocking pieces only on cells nobody stands on, so a seat is about as likely to be occupied as a bare cell and tells nothing about who sits there. — `Floorplan::blocks()`, `Floorplan::spareBlocks()`, `Furniture::canHoldSomeone()`, `ClueDraw::BLOCKING`, `CaseGenerator`
- **MY4** The murderer is the only suspect in the victim's room, which holds exactly 2 people. — `Floorplan::murdererIn()`, `Investigation::murderer()`
- **MY5** Every case has exactly one solution, proven by the generator before the case opens, and exactly N clues, one per person. The first one is always "{victim} was alone with the murderer" (MY4). Every other clue is a card anchored on one suspect, a different one each time, and holds one or two facts. The first fact is about that suspect:
  - where they are: in a room, on a seat, in a row, in a column, on a corner square of the grid (one of its 4 corners), in a corner of a room (the square touches another room or the grid edge both to the north or south and to the east or west);
  - furniture: next to a piece, or next to no piece of a kind that stands in their room; in a room with a piece of a kind; what stands on the square just north, south, east or west or exactly n squares away in that direction (same column or same row, whatever stands between, walls included; never another person, since nobody shares a row or a column); further north, south, east or west than a piece of a kind (strictly: at least one such piece stands on a row further south, further north, a column further west or further east, whatever the column or row); the only person on that kind of seat (armchair, sofa or bed; nobody else on the plan, victim included, sits on one);
  - company: alone (nobody else in their room), alone with a woman or a man (exactly one other person in their room, of that gender), sharing a room with a woman or a man on a kind of seat, sharing a room with someone next to a kind of furniture (always another suspect, never themselves);
  - others: sharing a room with someone, on the same diagonal as someone (as many rows apart as columns apart), further north or further west than someone, exactly n rows further north or exactly n columns further west than someone.

  The second fact, when there is one, is either another fact of a different kind about the same suspect (written with "he"/"she", or with their name again when the first fact names someone else of the same gender), or, after "alone with a woman/man", a fact about the square of that companion (a seat, next to furniture or to none of a kind, a row, a column, a grid or room corner, furniture n squares away, further north, south, east or west than a piece; never their room nor what it holds), or a room fact: a room was empty, held exactly n people, or held at least n people (n from 2, never the suspect's own room). Room facts only ever come second. A second fact about the same square is dropped when one of the two follows from the other on every square of the plan ("the only person in an armchair" counts as "in an armchair", "a man on a bed in his room" or "someone else beside a lamp in her room" as "a bed, a lamp in their room"), so a card never repeats itself; it is also dropped when the two facts together leave fewer than 2 free squares for the suspect on the plan, so a card never hands out a square on its own. No fact names the victim or describes the victim's room: no head count of it, nobody is "alone with" anyone there, and "in a room with", "sharing a room with a woman/man on" and "sharing a room with someone next to" are never said of someone in it. "Next to" means sharing a side with the person's square, in the same room: a wall in between or a corner does not count. The cards come in the order of the deduction: read one after the other, each one narrows the board from what the previous ones settled, and the victim is placed last or second to last; a case whose search runs out of time is never accepted. A room's head count includes the victim. Rows are numbered 1 to N from the north and columns lettered A to N from the west. — `Solver`, `ClueDraw` (`givesAway()`), `Clue::square()`, `Deduction`, `ClueKind`, `Clue` (`then`, `companion`), `ClueKind::isGiven()`, `Floorplan::isBeside()`, `Floorplan::isRoomCorner()`, `Floorplan::furnishes()`
- **MY6** The victim's clue is revealed as soon as the case opens, without any task and whatever the toggle of MY1. While Moss Mysteries is on, each rewarded completion reveals the next hidden clue of the open case, in a fixed order: a task, and the parent it completes (S3), reveal one each, and widget completions count like any other. A task reveals at most one clue per case: reopening it hides nothing and completing it again reveals nothing more. Nothing is revealed once every clue is known, without an open case, or after the case is solved. The completion tells how many clues it revealed. — `RevealClues`, `Investigation::revealFor()`, `Investigation::revealedClues()`, `CompleteTaskHandler`, `CompletionView`
- **MY7** A player has one open case at a time. A new case opens only on request, once the previous one is solved (otherwise `CaseStillOpen`), with every clue hidden but the victim's (and the gift of MY12) and empty notes; opening one does not depend on the toggle of MY1. Cases are kept after they are solved: the latest one is shown until a new one opens. — `OpenInvestigationHandler`, `CaseStillOpen`, `InvestigationRepository::latestOf()`, unique index `investigation_owner_open` (migration `Version20261031094947`). Cases open when these rules arrived were drawn again under them at the same size, with as many clues revealed as before (the victim's given clue counting as one, so at least that one and at most all N) and empty notes, keeping their gift and opening date; solved cases were left as they were (migration `Version20261031132711`, which also adds `genders` and `rules` to every case, the solved ones getting the gender of each name and no rules)
- **MY8** An accusation (`POST /api/mystery/investigations/{id}/accusation`) names the case it is about, where everyone stands and who the murderer is, and is checked against the solution as a whole. A case that is not the open one any more (solved, or replaced by a new one opened on another device) is not found: nothing changes, so a device still showing an old case never gets a wrong accusation for the new one. A wrong accusation changes nothing, costs nothing and never says which part is wrong (`WrongAccusation`). A right one solves the case at once: only then are the solution and the murderer sent to the player, and the board is kept as the final notes. — `Investigation::accuse()`, `AccuseHandler`, `WrongAccusation`
- **MY9** Pencil marks, who is placed where and which cells are crossed out, are saved with the case on the server (`PUT /api/mystery/investigations/{id}/notes`), so a phone and an iPad show the same board. Notes name their case and a case that is not the open one any more is not found, so a device still showing an old case cannot overwrite the marks of the new one. Notes must fit the board: one entry per person, every cell inside the board, no cell crossed twice and no cell shared by two people (`InvalidNotes`). A solved case takes no more notes. — `Investigation::takeNotes()`, `TakeNotesHandler`, `InvalidNotes`
- **MY10** Two achievements, **first_case** for the first case solved and **ten_cases** for 10, are checked after a right accusation (G9). — `FirstCase`, `TenCases`, `PlayerStats::$casesSolved`, `DoctrinePlayerStatsLedger`, `AccuseHandler`
- **MY11** Changes to the open case made at the same moment (a task completed on one device while notes are saved on another, an accusation next to a reveal) are applied one after the other, each starting from what the previous one saved, like GH11. Two simultaneous requests to open a case end with a single case. — `InvestigationRepository::openLockedOf()`, `AtomicChange`, unique index `investigation_owner_open`
- **MY12** A player's first case is a gift: it opens with every clue revealed, so it can be solved at once, and completed tasks reveal nothing more on it. There is one gift per player, whatever the toggle of MY1 does: switching Moss Mysteries off and on again gives no other. Players whose case was open before the gift existed had it completed (migration `Version20261031120103`); those without an open case get the gift with their next one. — `Investigation::open()` (`gift`), `InvestigationRepository::receivedGift()`, `OpenInvestigationHandler`
- **MY13** Besides its N cards, a case may state 0, 1 or 2 rules about the whole board, always visible, sent with the case (`rules`) and never counted among the clues, revealed or hidden, nor touched by the gift (MY12). A rule is one of: how many people (victim included) sit on a kind of seat, exactly, at least or at most n; exactly n women or n men on a kind of seat; no room is empty; no room holds more than n people (n from 2, and some room other than the victim's holds exactly n); a room is empty; every woman, or every man, shares a room with someone of the other gender. Rules are true, drawn before the cards from the ones that hold, a different kind each, preferring those that already narrow the board; the cards then come in the order of the deduction with the rules known from the start (MY5). A rule never gives the murderer away on its own: nothing is said about seats that all stand in the victim's room, the room cap is set by another room, and "every woman/man shares a room with someone of the other gender" is never said of the victim's gender. — `ClueDraw::rules()`, `ClueKind::isRule()`, `Bound`, `Clue` (`bound`), `Investigation::rules()`, `CaseView`
