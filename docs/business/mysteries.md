# Moss Mysteries

An opt-in logic puzzle fed by completed tasks. A generated board hides a murder: everyone stands somewhere, one person is the victim and the murderer is the only other person in the victim's room. The clues that locate people stay hidden until tasks are completed, one clue per task. Solving a case unlocks achievements only (no XP, no dew). In the code a game is an `Investigation` (the `Mystery` context); `Case` is a reserved word in PHP.

- **MY1** Moss Mysteries is off by default and switched on in Settings › Preferences (`PUT /api/me/mysteries` `{enabled}`, saved on the account and in the session as `mysteries`, so it survives a reload). While it is off the Mysteries entry is hidden from the navigation and no clue is revealed, but an open case is kept as it is. — `User::playMysteries()`, `ChooseMysteriesHandler`, `SecurityUser`, `RevealClues`
- **MY2** A case is an N×N board, N drawn between 6 and 14, split into contiguous rooms of at least 2 cells (between ⌈N/2⌉ and ⌈0.8·N⌉ rooms), each of a different kind (kitchen, living room, bedroom…). N people live on it: N−1 suspects and 1 victim, known from the start. — `CaseGenerator`, `Floorplan`, `RoomKind`, `Blueprint`
- **MY3** Exactly one person stands on each row and on each column. Armchair, sofa and bed can hold a person; plant, books, bath, toilet, tv, piano, lamp, stove and washer block their cell, nobody stands there. The generator puts seats on any free cell and blocking pieces only on cells nobody stands on, so a seat is about as likely to be occupied as a bare cell and tells nothing about who sits there. — `Floorplan::blocks()`, `Furniture::canHoldSomeone()`, `CaseGenerator`
- **MY4** The murderer is the only suspect in the victim's room, which holds exactly 2 people. — `Floorplan::murdererIn()`, `Investigation::murderer()`
- **MY5** Every case has exactly one solution, proven by the generator before the case opens. A clue says where someone is (in a room, on a piece of furniture, next to a piece of furniture, in a row, in a column), who shares a room with whom, who is further north or further west than whom, which room is empty or holds exactly n people. "Next to" means sharing a side with the person's square, even through a wall into another room; corners do not count. A room's head count includes the victim. Rows are numbered 1 to N from the north and columns lettered A to N from the west. — `Solver`, `ClueDraw`, `ClueKind`, `Clue`
- **MY6** While Moss Mysteries is on, each rewarded completion reveals the next hidden clue of the open case, in a fixed order: a task, and the parent it completes (S3), reveal one each, and widget completions count like any other. A task reveals at most one clue per case: reopening it hides nothing and completing it again reveals nothing more. Nothing is revealed once every clue is known, without an open case, or after the case is solved. The completion tells how many clues it revealed. — `RevealClues`, `Investigation::revealFor()`, `CompleteTaskHandler`, `CompletionView`
- **MY7** A player has one open case at a time. A new case opens only on request, once the previous one is solved (otherwise `CaseStillOpen`), with every clue hidden and empty notes; opening one does not depend on the toggle of MY1. Cases are kept after they are solved: the latest one is shown until a new one opens. — `OpenInvestigationHandler`, `CaseStillOpen`, `InvestigationRepository::latestOf()`, unique index `investigation_owner_open` (migration `Version20261031094947`)
- **MY8** An accusation (`POST /api/mystery/investigations/{id}/accusation`) names the case it is about, where everyone stands and who the murderer is, and is checked against the solution as a whole. A case that is not the open one any more (solved, or replaced by a new one opened on another device) is not found: nothing changes, so a device still showing an old case never gets a wrong accusation for the new one. A wrong accusation changes nothing, costs nothing and never says which part is wrong (`WrongAccusation`). A right one solves the case at once: only then are the solution and the murderer sent to the player, and the board is kept as the final notes. — `Investigation::accuse()`, `AccuseHandler`, `WrongAccusation`
- **MY9** Pencil marks, who is placed where and which cells are crossed out, are saved with the case on the server (`PUT /api/mystery/investigations/{id}/notes`), so a phone and an iPad show the same board. Notes name their case and a case that is not the open one any more is not found, so a device still showing an old case cannot overwrite the marks of the new one. Notes must fit the board: one entry per person, every cell inside the board, no cell crossed twice and no cell shared by two people (`InvalidNotes`). A solved case takes no more notes. — `Investigation::takeNotes()`, `TakeNotesHandler`, `InvalidNotes`
- **MY10** Two achievements, **first_case** for the first case solved and **ten_cases** for 10, are checked after a right accusation (G9). — `FirstCase`, `TenCases`, `PlayerStats::$casesSolved`, `DoctrinePlayerStatsLedger`, `AccuseHandler`
- **MY11** Changes to the open case made at the same moment (a task completed on one device while notes are saved on another, an accusation next to a reveal) are applied one after the other, each starting from what the previous one saved, like GH11. Two simultaneous requests to open a case end with a single case. — `InvestigationRepository::openLockedOf()`, `AtomicChange`, unique index `investigation_owner_open`
