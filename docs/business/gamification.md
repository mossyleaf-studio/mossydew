# Gamification

- **G1** Completing a task earns a reward once, kept on the task (its XP and dew). **Reopening it takes that reward back** (XP, dew, and the task counted in an active shared goal), so completing it again earns it again, at that day's streak. Losing XP can drop the level, but crossing a level reached before brings no new level-up nor species: only the highest level reached counts. Tasks rewarded before rewards were kept on the task (2026-10-17) keep their reward when reopened and earn nothing again. Deleting a task never removes XP. A subtask is a task: it earns its own reward, and its parent earns one too when the last subtask completes it (S3); reopening the subtask reopens the parent and takes both back (S4). — `Task::claimReward()`, `Task::forfeitReward()`, `Player::forfeit()`, `ReopenTaskHandler`
- **G2** Base XP by quadrant: Plant 25, Water 20, unsorted 8, Trim 10, Compost 5 (Plant pays most: important work done before it is urgent). +5 when done on or before its deadline. — `RewardPolicy`
- **G3** Streak multiplier: × (1 + 0.05 × streak days, capped at 10 days). — `RewardPolicy`
- **G4** Streak: consecutive days (user's time zone) with at least one rewarded completion. Missing a day resets the shown streak to 0; the best streak is kept. — `Streak`
- **G5** Every level costs the same 150 XP: level n is reached at 150·(n−1) XP (L2 = 150, L3 = 300, L4 = 450…), so a new species always stays a handful of tasks away. — `LevelCurve`
- **G6** No punishment: collected species, achievements and the best streak are never lost. Only reopening a task takes back what completing it gave (G1); dew already spent leaves the balance below zero until new tasks refill it.
- **G7** Herbarium: crossing a level while completing a task collects one moss species per level crossed, drawn at random among the species of the catalogue not collected yet, weighted by rarity; it is revealed in the level-up celebration. A greenhouse expedition draws one the same way (GH7). Once every species is collected, levels bring no more species. Levels already reached before the herbarium existed were each given one species when it was introduced. — `SpeciesCatalog`, `SpeciesDraw`, `CollectSpecies`
- **G7b** Rarity follows how often a species is observed on iNaturalist (snapshot of 2026-10-07): common from 15,000 observations (weight 8), uncommon from 7,000 (4), rare from 2,500 (2), very rare below (1). A species with weight 1 is eight times less likely to be drawn than a common one. Collected species are numbered in the order they were found. — `Rarity`, `SpeciesDraw`
- **G7c** Reaching a shared goal with friends also collects one species, for every member (SG4 in [social.md](social.md)).
- **G8** Each species shows an iNaturalist photo under CC0 or CC BY, credited with its author and licence on the card and on `/credits`, and a short note written for MossyDew (`speciesNotes.json`). Photos and common names are imported once with `bin/console app:herbarium:import` and served by MossyDew, never loaded from iNaturalist at runtime. — `ImportSpeciesCommand`
- **G9** Achievements unlock once, checked after completing and sorting in the matrix, after upgrading a facility or coming back from an expedition in the greenhouse, and after solving a Moss Mysteries case (MY10). Newly unlocked ones are celebrated once, then marked seen. — `AchievementCheck`, `AchievementRule` classes
