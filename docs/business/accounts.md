# Accounts

- **A1** People sign in with their **mossyleaf account** (`accounts.mossyleaf.studio`, shared by every mossyleaf app). There is no sign-up, password or invitation in MossyDew: accounts are invited, reset their password and manage passkeys in mossyleaf accounts, and only members of its `mossydew` group may open MossyDew (others see "Your mossyleaf account does not have access to MossyDew yet."). — `OidcSingleSignOn`, `AccountsAuthenticator`
- **A1b** On sign-in the account (OIDC `sub`) is matched to its user; a user from before mossyleaf accounts (no account yet) is linked once by the **same email** and keeps all their data; otherwise the first sign-in creates the user (name from the account, or the part of the email before @; time zone from `zoneinfo` or Europe/Paris), their player profile (0 XP, empty herbarium) and their greenhouse, opened small with no dew and 2 empty pots (GH1). A later email change in the account follows on the next sign-in. An email already used by another linked user is refused. — `SignInHandler`
- **A2** Signing out of MossyDew also ends the mossyleaf accounts session, then comes back to MossyDew (which asks to sign in again); a session restored by remember-me has no ID token to show mossyleaf accounts, so it ends on the mossyleaf accounts page instead. A sign-in link (`state` + PKCE verifier) works once, for the browser that started it. — `SignOutOfAccounts`, `AccountsSession`, `SignInAttempts`
- **A3** Each user sees only their own data (owner scoping). Another user's task or project id answers 404. — `OwnerScope`
- **A4** The time zone decides when "today" starts and ends; it can be changed in settings. — `User::today()`, `ChangeTimezoneHandler`
- **A5** Each user may choose the app's **background** and **accent** colours (Settings › Appearance): one of five themes — Moss `#f5f5f3`/`#5b7f3a` (the default), Terracotta `#f7f2ec`/`#b0532c`, Fjord `#eef2f5`/`#2f6a8f`, Plum `#f6f2f6`/`#7a4d84`, Night `#1d201b`/`#93bb6c` — or any `#rrggbb` pair, saved on their account (`PUT /api/me/theme` `{background, accent}`, lowercased; anything else is refused) and applied to every page they open (`--theme-background`/`--theme-accent` on `<html>`, `color-scheme: dark` when the background is dark, and `theme` in the session). Until they choose, the Moss default applies. — `Theme`, `User::wear()`, `ChooseThemeHandler`, `base.html.twig`
- **A5b** Independently of the colours, each user picks a **design** — classic (the default: Patua One and Inter, thin borders, small radii) or cozy (muhmeal's look: Fredoka and Nunito, thick borders, round shapes, buttons with a lip that presses down) — and an **icon set** — Lucide (the default) or Phosphor (duotone for navigation and decoration, bold for actions). Both are saved on their account (`PUT /api/me/design` `{design: classic|cozy}`, `PUT /api/me/icons` `{icons: lucide|phosphor}`; anything else is refused), apply at once and on every page (`data-design="cozy"` on `<html>`, `design` and `icons` in the session). — `Design`, `IconSet`, `User::decorate()`, `User::drawWith()`, `ChooseDesignHandler`, `ChooseIconsHandler`, `assets/vue/icons.js`
